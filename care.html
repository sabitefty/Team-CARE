About Us

WE ARE
TEAM C.A.R.E

Team C.A.R.E is Bangladesh’s first nationally and internationally recognized student-led assistive technology innovation team formed by students from Notre Dame College and Adamjee Cantonment College, Dhaka, dedicated to developing intelligent and affordable solutions for physically challenged individuals. The team works on research and innovation in assistive technology, with the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair serving as its flagship project — an AI and IoT-based smart wheelchair designed to enhance mobility, safety, and independence through modern technology.

Our Work
FEATURED PROJECTS

The journey of the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair began nearly two years ago after witnessing the real-life mobility struggles of a paralysed family member. Since then, Team C.A.R.E has been continuously researching and developing affordable intelligent assistive technology solutions aimed at improving mobility, independence, and quality of life for physically challenged individuals in Bangladesh. Today, according to the World Health Organization, over 1.3 billion people, nearly 16% of the world’s population — live with disabilities, many of whom still face major mobility limitations and lack access to affordable and advanced assistive mobility technology, especially in developing countries like Bangladesh.

  • AI & IoT-Based Smart Wheelchair System
  • Head Movement Controlled Navigation
  • Voice Command-Based Control
  • Finger Gesture Control Support
  • Real-Time Health Monitoring System
  • Mobile App Connectivity & Remote Monitoring
  • Home Automation Integration
  • Obstacle Detection & Safety System
  • Emergency Alert & Assistance Feature
  • Affordable Assistive Technology Solution
  • ECG Signal-Based Research Integration
  • Smart Battery & Power Management System

TEAM ROADMAP
2022

Team Formation

Team CARE was founded by a group of passionate engineering students with a vision to make assistive technology affordable and accessible in Bangladesh.

Completed

First Prototype — CARE Wheelchair v1.0

Built and tested the first functional prototype of the CARE Wheelchair with basic IoT features and manual controls.

Completed

Awards & Recognition

Won multiple national competitions, published our first IEEE paper, and received the Bangladesh ICT Ministry innovation grant.

Launched v2.0 with full AI obstacle detection, voice control, and IoT dashboard. Representing Bangladesh internationally.
