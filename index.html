The journey of the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair began nearly two years ago after witnessing the real-life mobility struggles of a paralysed family member. Since then, Team C.A.R.E has been continuously researching and developing affordable intelligent assistive technology solutions aimed at improving mobility, independence, and quality of life for physically challenged individuals in Bangladesh.

OUR ROADMAP
MAY 2025

TEAM FORMATION

Team C.A.R.E was founded by a group of passionate students from Adamjee Cantonment College with a vision to develop smart, affordable, and accessible assistive technology for people with disabilities in Bangladesh.

✓ Completed
MAY 2025

FIRST PROTOTYPE — C.A.R.E WHEELCHAIR V1.0

Designed and developed the first functional prototype of the C.A.R.E Wheelchair featuring hand gesture control, basic IoT integration, and smart mobility assistance systems.

✓ Completed
LATE 2025

ADVANCED CONTROL SYSTEM & IMPROVEMENTS

Upgraded the wheelchair with head gesture control, improved responsiveness, enhanced safety systems, and smarter user interaction features for better accessibility and independence.

✓ Completed
EARLY 2026

C.A.R.E WHEELCHAIR V2.0 + AI INTEGRATION

Developed the advanced V2.0 prototype featuring AI-powered obstacle detection, voice interaction, smart navigation assistance, live monitoring, and an IoT-based dashboard system.

✓ Completed
2026 — NOW

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Currently focused on improving autonomous mobility, AI accuracy, real-world usability, and intelligent assistive systems while seeking sponsorships, partnerships, and research opportunities.

⚡ In Progress
2027

COMMERCIAL LAUNCH & SMART MOBILITY

Planning to commercially launch the C.A.R.E Wheelchair in Bangladesh with upgraded smart mobility features, improved accessibility, and affordable assistive technology solutions for real-world users.

◎ Vision
2027+

BRAIN SIGNAL CONTROL & NEXT-GEN AI

Researching advanced assistive technologies where wheelchairs can interact with human brain signals and neural responses, enabling smarter and more independent mobility systems through AI, neuroscience, and robotics.

◎ Vision
FUTURE GOAL

C.A.R.E RESEARCH LAB & GLOBAL EXPANSION

Establishing the C.A.R.E Research Lab to develop next-generation healthcare, AI, robotics, and assistive technologies while representing Bangladesh globally through innovation, research, and impactful engineering solutions.

About Us

WE ARE
TEAM C.A.R.E

Team C.A.R.E is Bangladesh's first nationally and internationally recognized student-led assistive technology innovation team formed by students from Notre Dame College and Adamjee Cantonment College, Dhaka, dedicated to developing intelligent and affordable solutions for physically challenged individuals. The team works on research and innovation in assistive technology, with the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair serving as its flagship project — an AI and IoT-based smart wheelchair designed to enhance mobility, safety, and independence.
